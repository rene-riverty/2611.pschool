Slide 5 · Strategy

Problem · value · hypothesis

Problem statement hook

Not setting up a sustainable payment plan leads to losing benchmark position — cashflow and client trust erode while avoidable agent rework and consumer avoidance scale.

Case: Collection — instalment plan. Crisis if nothing for 12 months: less cashflow than possible — loss of benchmark position or customer satisfaction. Systemic avoidance (wait / close tab / call later), not an edge case.

Value proposition

For consumers like Mara who cannot clear a balance in one go, Riverty Collection offers a digital instalment journey that helps them agree to a plan they can actually keep — and hands them to an agent with full context when they cannot finish alone — so recovery stays responsible, self-service rises for real, and avoidable agent work falls.

Promise: sustainable digital resolution — not digital volume of agreements.

B4 VP: Collection Agents get a read-only, one-screen handoff (identity, affordability, last step, plan + why, freshness) at case-open — cutting repeated identity/affordability checks 69.7% → ≤55% without harming SSR ≥34.7%.

Data-backed hypothesis

Based on N-08 (“Half my calls start with me asking for the same details…”) and M3 evidence that 69.7% of agent contacts repeat identity/affordability checks—fed by mid-journey stalls such as Income document→Plan review (−22.1%)—I believe that solving the blind handoff for Daniel, the context-blind Collection Agent, will result in faster, keepable resolution instead of voice restart, as measured by a drop in repeated identity/affordability checks from 69.7% to ≤55% (~15 pp). I will protect SSR resolved % of journey starters (baseline 34.7%) and will make a go/no-go decision after 8 weeks.

PRD highlights · B4

  • Vision: One-screen, read-only handoff — start where Mara stopped, not at zero.
  • Problem: Blind handoff — 69.7% re-ask (N-08; N-09 “just a number”).
  • Success: 69.7% → ≤55%; SSR ≥34.7%; 8-week window.
  • US-1–3: Intake context · plan + why · freshness + instrumentation.
  • 3 screens: Case list → Handoff Summary → Confirmation.
  • MUST / WON'T: RO + instrumentation; no write-path / chat / multi-claim.
  • Safety: Missing = Unavailable; no AI “suggest why.”

Slide 10 · Story

Friction · aha · next

Friction points

  • Blind handoff — workspace hides the online attempt (N-08)
  • Voice restart — rebuild identity + affordability by phone
  • “Just a number” — failed plan with no why (N-09)
  • Trust gap — no freshness → agent won’t rely on data
  • Habit inertia — panel ships, re-asks continue

Aha moment

Daniel opens the case, sees Mara’s identity + affordability + last step + plan why on one screen, and realizes: I don’t have to start at zero — I can continue where she stopped.

First no_reask mark = the hypnosis click; that’s when Engagement becomes real.

Key takeaways

  • Release + M-tier launch — success = Engagement, not awareness
  • Audience = Daniel / pilot agents — workspace + floor, not paid
  • Silence kills the habit test (~1.8k re-ask contacts/mo pattern)
  • Metrics: shown ≥90% · ≤20s · 69.7%→≤55% · SSR ≥34.7%
  • Bad signal → fix enablement/trust, not more channels
  • Most likely: Iterate; double-down only on clean week-8 read

What I’d do next

  1. Name owners (Eng, Pilot TL, Analyst, Compliance) — unblock sign-off
  2. Lock metric definitions + Monday strip (manual OK until dashboard)
  3. Phase 1 beta (5–8 agents): brief, dry-run, instrumentation check
  4. Launch moment: TL huddle + in-product on; day-0 adoption check
  5. Week 4 / 8 reads: iterate or double-down / kill — no write-path scope creep
